# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **MRN** | Medical record number: the patient's number in the hospital. |
| **Encounter** | One stay or visit of a patient (emergency visit, admission, day case). Orders, results and notes hang off it. |
| **MAR** | Medication administration record: what was given to the patient, when and by whom. |
| **NEWS2** | National Early Warning Score 2: a score from the vital signs that flags a deteriorating patient. 5 to 6 needs urgent review, 7 or more is an emergency. |
| **ESI** | Emergency Severity Index: five-level triage scale, 1 is the most urgent. |
| **STAT** | Needed immediately. |
| **HGT** | Bedside blood glucose test (mg/dL). |
| **SBAR** | Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation: the structure of a clinical handover. |
| **Break-glass** | Emergency access to a chart outside your own unit, with a justification and a review afterwards. |
| **Critical result** | A laboratory value dangerous enough that a clinician must acknowledge it. |
| **WHO checklist** | The World Health Organization's surgical safety checklist. |
| **Aldrete score** | Recovery score used to decide when a patient can leave the recovery room. |
| **PACU** | Post-anaesthesia care unit (recovery room). |
| **Morse / Braden** | Nursing scales for fall risk and pressure-injury risk. |
| **NSSF** | Lebanon's National Social Security Fund, one of the payers the billing supports. |
