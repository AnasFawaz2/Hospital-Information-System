# Roadmap (ideas, not promises)

- **Nurse task list from care plans:** scheduled observations, repositioning, specimen rounds and handover tasks, next to doses and lines.
- **Special units:** dialysis, cardiac catheterisation lab, endoscopy, burns, psychiatry.
- **A dashboard for every role:** emergency, pharmacy, laboratory, radiology, management and billing, in the same simple card-and-list style.
- **More languages:** Arabic and French next to English.
- **Integration:** standard interfaces to laboratory and imaging equipment and to insurers.
